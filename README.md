# Joohyeong Park (박주형)

I'm a fourth-year undergraduate in the Department of Intelligent Systems & Robotics at Chungbuk National University (CBNU) and a member of SUV LAB. I plan to go on to a master's program, and I'm aiming for an R&D job in aerospace and defense in 2028.

<sub>Korean names that have no official English name I could confirm are given with my own translation. Paper titles are kept in Korean, with a translation in italics.</sub>

## Research interests

- **Swarm trajectory planning** — I work on algorithms that generate formation-transition trajectories for drone shows before the flight: when many drones move to the next formation, the transition should be collision-free and take minimum time.
- **Multi-drone flight systems** — In multi-drone light shows built on PX4 and GPS-RTK, I have worked on trajectory generation, flight-safety verification and real flights. As part of a team, I have flown real flights with up to 36 drones.
- **Online swarm exploration** — I take part in joint research on multiple drones exploring and planning paths in dynamic environments.

Tools I use: C / C++, Python, ROS2, PX4, PX4 SITL, Gazebo, GPS-RTK, Git, Linux

## Publications

Papers presented at conferences.

- **GeoSelective: 기하학 인식 기반 선택적 충돌 회피를 통한 드론 군집 포메이션 전환**\
  *GeoSelective: drone swarm formation transition through geometry-aware selective collision avoidance*\
  박주형 · 전진형 · 문성태 — KCC 2026 (2026 한국컴퓨터종합학술대회), first author\
  Formation transition through analysis of geometric collision patterns and selective detours, with a quantitative comparison of an approach combined with CAT-ORA. Excellence award (우수상), undergraduate division of the undergraduate/junior paper competition.
- **군집 드론쇼 운영을 위한 안전한 궤적 생성 성능 비교 및 분석**\
  *Comparison and analysis of safe trajectory generation performance for operating swarm drone shows*\
  박주형 · 전진형 · 문성태 — KSC 2025 (2025 한국소프트웨어종합학술대회), first author\
  Combines a fair Hungarian algorithm with Ego-Swarm and compares its formation-transition performance with a layer-separation approach in simulation.
- **궤적 안전성을 고려한 군집 드론쇼 시스템 개발**\
  *Development of a swarm drone show system considering trajectory safety*\
  전진형 · 박주형 · 문성태 — KSC 2025 (2025 한국소프트웨어종합학술대회), second author\
  Joint research on a platform that links PX4 SITL, Gazebo and ROS2 for safety-distance checks and visual verification.
